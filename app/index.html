Read this before you play

  • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Do not use cheats on your platinum save.
  • There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. A story mission can only be played when it first appears.
  • Save manually at the start of every chapter and before the final mission. That manual save is your only way back if you miss something.
  • One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude — the Lucia balcony meditation spot in Chapter 1 disappears when Chapter 1 ends. Do it in Chapter 1.

  1. Stage 1 — Story, Chapters 1–4

    Play the story in order: Gone Fishing, then Chapters 1 to 4 and the final mission. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation as soon as you can. Tick each story trophy as it pops.

  2. Stage 2 — Stranger mission strands

    Clear all 8 stranger strands — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and the rest — to their final missions. Each strand ends in its own trophy, and all 8 together earn Strangers & Freaks.

  3. Stage 3 — Collectibles

    Finish the 3 card sets, WAiNK at 100 locations, discover 100 vehicles, explore all 52 areas, buy all 8 garages and register 10 vehicles on the Scooter Bros app.

  4. Stage 4 — Activities

    Gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges, win the 14 drone races and 20 different races, complete 250 stunts, meditate at the remaining spots, and try all 9 activity types.

  5. Stage 5 — Crime and money

    Rob the shops, jewelry store, armored trucks, 5 bank vaults and one illegal operation per gang; launder $250,000 of marked bills and spend $100,000 across shops.

  6. Stage 6 — 100% and platinum

    Check the in-game completion percentage, mop up whatever is left, and earn A Long Road for 100% completion — then Vice Got a Price! follows.

Missable trophies

Only one trophy is missable from the official descriptions. Everything else can be earned in free roam after the story — but because there is no mission replay, keep a manual save at the start of each chapter anyway.

Story trophies are not missable — but they are one-shot

Chapter and mission trophies unlock in story order and cannot be missed by playing through. They also cannot be replayed, so if you ever worry you skipped something, reload the manual save from the start of that chapter.
