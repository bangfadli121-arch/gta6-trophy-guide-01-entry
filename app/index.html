Read this before you play

  • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Do not use cheats on your platinum save.
  • There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. A story mission can only be played when it first appears.
  • Save manually at the start of every chapter and before the final mission. That manual save is your only way back if you miss something.
  • One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude — the Lucia balcony meditation spot in Chapter 1 disappears when Chapter 1 ends. Do it in Chapter 1.

↓ Scroll — Stage 1, Stage 2 and Stage 3 below

  1. Stage 1 — Story first

    Play the story in order: Gone Fishing, then Chapters 1 to 4 and the final mission, plus the stranger strands you meet along the way — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, each ending in its own trophy. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation as soon as you can. Tick each story trophy as it pops.

  2. Stage 2 — Activities & collectibles

    The counted grind: 250 stunts, gold on all 42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone races, 20 different races, the 3 card sets, WAiNK at 100 locations, 100 vehicles discovered, all 52 areas explored, 8 garages bought, 10 vehicles on the Scooter Bros app, and the rest of the numbered targets. Work through them with the checklist — every one shows its PSN target.

  3. Stage 3 — Side & cleanup

    The one-off trophies — the jewelry store, the armored truck, the 5 bank vaults, one illegal operation per gang, the $250,000 of laundered marked bills, the $100,000 spent across shops, and the odd encounters like the Rhino and the showroom smash. Then check the in-game completion percentage, mop up what is left, and earn A Long Road for 100% completion — then Vice Got a Price! follows.

Missable trophies

Only one trophy is missable from the official descriptions. Everything else can be earned in free roam after the story — but because there is no mission replay, keep a manual save at the start of each chapter anyway.

Story trophies are not missable — but they are one-shot

Chapter and mission trophies unlock in story order and cannot be missed by playing through. They also cannot be replayed, so if you ever worry you skipped something, reload the manual save from the start of that chapter.
